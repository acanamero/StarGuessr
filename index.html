About

Hi! I'm Alejandro Cañamero, a physics graduate currently doing a master's in astrophysics at the Universidad de La Laguna (ULL). I made StarGuessr to make studying stellar spectra a little more fun.

There are only 28 spectra for now, but I'll keep adding more.

Credits

Spectra: all spectra come from the MELCHIORS database, the Mercator Library of High Resolution Stellar Spectroscopy (Royer et al. 2024, A&A 681, A107), observed with the HERMES spectrograph on the Mercator telescope. Full credit for the data goes to the MELCHIORS team.

Line criteria: the classification criteria and explanations follow the course notes Atmósferas Estelares, Tema 1 · Líneas de diagnóstico y clases de luminosidad by Sara Rodríguez Berlanas and Artemio Herrero, based on Maíz Apellániz, Negueruela & Caballero (2024, arXiv:2410.07301) and Gray & Corbally (2009). How to play StarGuessr

Each round shows a real rectified spectrum (3900–6800 Å) from the MELCHIORS library. Read the absorption lines and give its MK spectral type.

  • Exact type: 100 pts. Within one subtype (e.g. B2 for a B3; for G stars, the neighbouring standard subtype of G0, G2, G5, G8, K0): accepted for 70 pts, with an explanation of why it isn't quite that type.
  • Miss: you get the real type and the line ratios that give it away. The star returns later in the run so you can try it again.
  • Expert mode also grades the luminosity class (I–V): +50 exact, +25 one class off.
  • Line IDs overlay the diagnostic lines but cost 30% of the round's points. Streaks add bonus points.
  • Clear all 28 stars to finish the run.

New to this? Download the study sheet (criteria for each class, line list, suffixes and a self-quiz) before you play.

Criteria follow the course notes Atmósferas Estelares, Tema 1 · Líneas de diagnóstico y clases de luminosidad by Sara Rodríguez Berlanas & Artemio Herrero (after Maíz Apellániz, Negueruela & Caballero 2024; Gray & Corbally 2009). Equivalent widths in the feedback are measured automatically on each spectrum and are approximate.
